      In-game overlay panels drawn over the game: what they show, where they go
      • Game PC only. Outrider runs the overlay window itself while the overlay is on (an Outrider on a server has none). It installs PyQt6, which the window needs, in its own venv the first time.
      • Play Elite borderless or windowed. The window sits over the game and lets every click through.
      • Arrange panels right over the game: drag to move, drag the corner to resize, mouse wheel for the background's transparency, Shift + wheel for the whole panel's, Ctrl + wheel for all panels' size. Done there or here ends it.
      • Alerts shows each alert switched on in Settings → Alerts (notify, sound or speak ticked) for a few seconds, danger longer and in red. The alerts are worded by this page: with no Outrider page open anywhere (a browser or the tablet), the panel stays empty.
